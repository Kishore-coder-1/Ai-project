System Architecture & AI Formulation (Group 16)

1. Exact Campus Spatial Graph

Models Blocks 25–38 (94 floors total) with entrance locations: separated buildings (25–30 and 31–32) linked by elevated 3rd-floor skybridges, joined structures (30–31, 32–35, and 36–37–38) linked internally from Floor 3 upwards, plus cross-road bridges (26 ↔ 38 and 28 ↔ 36–38).

2. Hard & Soft Accessibility Constraints

For Handicapped / Wheelchair profiles, staircases receive infinite cost (∞), ramp entries apply gentle-slope detour distances, and horizontal cruising speed reflects safe wheelchair pacing (1.4× travel time vs. walking). Lifts move between consecutive floors in 5s (25m shaft equivalent), while stairs take 20s (28m).

3. Admissible 3D Heuristic vs. Unweighted BFS

A* Search combines 2D planar distance with vertical floor delta to find the minimum-cost weighted path with far fewer node expansions than UCS, while BFS explores level-by-level in an unweighted graph to minimize total edge hops (often taking longer physical detours).

4. Contrastive Explainability

Compares the constrained accessible route against a shadow unconstrained student baseline to explain exact detours, lift selections, and avoided obstacles in plain English.
